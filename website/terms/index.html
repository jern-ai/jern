Terms of service

Terms in plain language.

These terms apply to every Jern Cloud workspace, on trial or on a paid pilot. A pilot order can add commercial details and controls for a specific team. If the order and this page disagree, the order wins.

Early service: Jern Cloud is an early commercial service. Do not use it for workloads that require certifications, contractual controls, or availability commitments that are not written into your pilot order.

The service

Jern Cloud runs the open-source Jern runtime in sessions on isolated machines against GitHub repositories you connect, under the policy those repositories or your workspace define, and delivers the result as pull requests. It includes the dashboard, the inference gateway, evidence storage, usage metering, and workspace administration. You administer your own repositories, branch protection, review rules, and, if you choose to bring one, your model provider account.

Trial and paid use

Every new workspace receives a 30-day trial with the full product. When the trial ends, existing sessions remain readable and new work is paused until a pilot order is agreed. Trials may be extended or ended at our discretion. Paid pilots are billed per workspace per month at the price in the order, with the included model allowance and the treatment of usage above it stated there. Model usage under your own provider key is billed by that provider, not by us.

Permitted use

You may use the service only for repositories and organizations you are authorized to administer, and only with content you have the right to process. You may not probe other tenants or the isolation boundary without written permission, interfere with the service, circumvent policy, budget, or rate limits, upload unlawful material, or use the service to violate another party's rights. Coordinated security testing is welcome by prior arrangement through security@jern.ai.

Your responsibilities

You decide what code, prompts, policies, and environment each session may use. You review every pull request before merging it. You protect your default branch, workflows, and policy baseline with your own repository controls. You keep your own backups. Jern does not guarantee that an agent will produce correct, secure, or non-infringing output; the evidence exists so that you can check.

Your content

You own your code, prompts, and the output of your sessions. You grant us the rights needed to run the service for you: to fetch the pinned revision, run the agent, store encrypted evidence, forward prompts to the model provider, and push to the branch the session owns. We do not use your content to train models, and we do not share it except with the processors listed in the privacy notice.

Availability and changes

The service is provided without a service-level agreement unless one is written into your pilot order. We may change features, limits, and prices as the product develops. Changes that materially reduce what an active paid pilot receives are communicated to workspace owners before they take effect, and a pilot may be cancelled in response without penalty for the remaining term.
