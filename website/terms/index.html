Founding Team pilot

Pilot terms in plain language.

These are the baseline terms for evaluating Jern Cloud during the paid design-partner phase. A pilot order or written agreement can add commercial details and controls for a specific team.

Pilot status: Jern Cloud is an early commercial service. Do not use it for workloads requiring certifications, contractual controls, or availability commitments that are not explicitly included in your pilot agreement.

The service

Jern Cloud provides GitHub-backed repository onboarding, run authorization, budget reservation, encrypted evidence retention, and dashboard access for the open-source Jern runtime. Customers provide and administer their own source repositories, GitHub runners, model-provider accounts, credentials, branch protection, environments, and review rules.

Permitted use

You may use the service for repositories and organizations you are authorized to administer. You may not probe other tenants, interfere with the service, bypass usage limits, upload unlawful material, or use the service to violate another party's rights.

Your responsibility

You decide what code, prompts, tools, policies, and secrets are available to a runner. You are responsible for reviewing generated changes, protecting branches and environments, maintaining backups, and assessing whether the service is appropriate for your data. Jern does not guarantee that an agent will produce correct, secure, or non-infringing output.

Fees and limits

The public Founding Team hypothesis is $250 per workspace per month for up to 10 governed repositories, 10 members, and a target 30-day evidence-retention period. Actual pilot dates, fees, included support, and limits are confirmed before paid activation. Provider and runner costs are not included.

Availability and changes

The pilot is provided without a service-level agreement unless one is written into the pilot agreement. Features and limits may change as the product develops. Material changes affecting an active paid pilot will be communicated before they take effect where practical.

Confidentiality and data

Jern will treat non-public customer information as confidential and use it to provide, secure, support, and improve the service. The privacy notice describes current processing. The security page describes the technical boundary and known limits.

Open source

The Jern runtime and Action are separately licensed under Apache-2.0. These pilot terms govern the hosted control plane and do not reduce rights granted by the open-source license.

Ending a pilot

Either side may end a pilot according to its written onboarding terms, or immediately for material misuse or security risk. The team should export any information it requires before termination. Deletion timing is confirmed during offboarding while automated retention controls are being completed.

Legal agreement

This public page is the baseline product description, not a substitute for entity, payment, liability, warranty, governing-law, and data-protection terms appropriate to a particular paid customer. Those terms must be completed in the pilot order before accepting payment.
